Rebuild Shirley Community Ambulance site in React

Replaces the GoDaddy Website Builder site with a Vite + React SPA.

Navigation goes from 13 entries to 5 by merging pages around visitor intent:
About (about/directors/photos), Volunteer (adult + student programs),
Services (CPR classes + stand-by requests), Support Us (donate + fundraisers),
and Contact. Members Only was a bare login wall, so it moved to the footer.
All former URLs redirect to their new location.

The old home page held only a donate banner and a heading repeated six times.
It now leads with who the agency is and the three actions people come for,
followed by capabilities, community programs, the fundraiser, and photos.

Also fixes: the "Signed in as: filler@godaddy.com" string that leaked into
every page, the absent "call 911" notice, a hardcoded copyright year, and
several typos in the body copy. Adds page titles, meta/OG tags, JSON-LD,
sitemap, robots.txt, keyboard-accessible accordion and lightbox, skip link,
and alt text throughout.

Content lives in src/data/site.js so it can be edited without touching
components. scripts/qa.mjs drives headless Chromium over every page to check
for console errors, broken images, and layout overflow.
